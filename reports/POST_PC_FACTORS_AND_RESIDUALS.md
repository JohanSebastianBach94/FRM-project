## 3) Notes on interpretation

- Blocks are **not forced** to produce PCs. If a block has <2 usable series after coverage/min-obs filtering, PCA is not possible.
- `method=empty` means the governed/frozen block exists but none of its series were usable in the Step-3 combined panel under current filters.
- More raw series does **not** automatically imply higher explained variance for PC1; it depends on correlation structure.
- Coverage grading in this report uses a simple heuristic: OK if post-dedupe usable series >= 3, WARN if 1–2, FAIL if 0/empty.

## 4) Residual/correlation quality signals (quick)

- `sr_abs_corr_median`/`p95` are absolute correlations across standardized residuals (last 250 obs). Lower is better for a pure univariate-GARCH prefilter, but not expected to be near zero in systemic stress regimes.
- Sigma eigenvalues should stay positive; very small/negative minima often indicate numerical PSD issues or extreme collinearity.
